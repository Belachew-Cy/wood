How this desk works

A unit is registered once with a category, the price the company paid and the place it lives. Allocating it names the department and, if one person is answerable, the custodian from the staff list; returning it puts the unit back on the available shelf and closes the allocation with a date. Deactivating a unit keeps its history but takes it off the live count.
