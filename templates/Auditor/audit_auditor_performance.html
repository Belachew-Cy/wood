Performance Measurement Guidelines

  • Calculate completion rate using completed assignments divided by eligible assignments.
  • Determine overdue status using due dates and actual assignment status.
  • Do not count cancelled assignments as completed work.
  • Use approved assignment records as the source of workload information.
  • Restrict individual performance information to authorized users.
  • Do not treat assignment volume alone as a measure of audit quality.
