Compliance Review Guidelines

  • Record the applicable policy, regulation or internal control requirement.
  • Identify the department responsible for compliance.
  • Maintain supporting evidence for each compliance assessment.
  • Document exceptions and link significant non-compliance to audit findings.
  • Schedule periodic reviews and retain assessment history.
  • Restrict changes to authorized audit personnel.
