Workload Management Rules

  • Calculate capacity from administrator-approved workload limits.
  • Detect overlapping assignment dates for the same auditor.
  • Exclude completed and cancelled assignments from active workload.
  • Do not automatically reassign auditors without authorization.
  • Record every assignment change in the existing audit activity log.
  • Restrict workload and personnel information according to audit permissions.
  • Do not classify an auditor as unavailable unless availability data supports that conclusion.
