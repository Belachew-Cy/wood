Audit Report Approval Process

1. Preparation

The assigned auditor prepares the report using verified audit evidence and findings.

2. Review

An authorized reviewer checks accuracy, completeness and supporting evidence.

3. Approval

The designated approving authority approves the report according to organizational policy.

4. Finalization

The approved report is retained with its version history and approval record.

Report Management Controls

  • Assign a unique reference number to each audit report.
  • Link every report to its corresponding audit plan.
  • Maintain report versions and revision history.
  • Restrict approval to authorized users.
  • Record reviewer comments and approval timestamps.
  • Prevent unauthorized modification of approved reports.
  • Maintain confidentiality and permission-controlled downloads.
