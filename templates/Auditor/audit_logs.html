Audit Log Security and Integrity

  • Audit logs should be generated automatically by server-side operations.
  • Users must not be allowed to modify or delete historical activity records.
  • Record the authenticated user, action, timestamp and relevant record reference.
  • Restrict access to sensitive IP addresses and security-event information.
  • Protect logs against unauthorized modification and deletion.
  • Use consistent timestamps and document the timezone.
  • Record failed access attempts and authorization failures where appropriate.
  • Apply the existing Audit Management permission system.
