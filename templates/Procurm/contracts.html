Contract Control and Compliance

  • Every contract must be linked to an existing supplier.
  • Contract approval must follow the existing organizational approval workflow.
  • Expired contracts must not be treated as active agreements.
  • Renewal reminders should be generated before the expiry date.
  • Contract amendments and renewals must retain the original audit history.
  • Only authorized users may view or download confidential contract documents.
  • Contract values and currencies must be validated before approval.
