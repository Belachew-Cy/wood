Control

A new job starts as a draft and authorises nothing by itself. Submitting it wakes the shared approval chain; the desks that sign see the client, the site, the delivery dates and the allocation the job will draw on. A signature starts the work and reserves that allocation, a refusal cancels the job, and a return comes back here for a revision before it is sent again.
