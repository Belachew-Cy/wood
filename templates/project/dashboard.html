How this desk works

A job is raised as a draft, given a manager, a team and the dates it has to hold. Submitting it opens the shared approval chain and freezes the figures while the desks sign; a signature starts the work and reserves the budget allocation through the same commitment the budget screen counts, and a refusal cancels the job without leaving money held. Milestones and tasks then move the job forward, and the register keeps one row the site and the accountant both read.
